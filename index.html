About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Leo J. Meehan School of Business at Stonehill College, where I teach courses in data analytics, business intelligence, and information systems. My research focuses on information exchange among multiple stakeholders, spatial decision support systems, and the application of GIS technology to support decision-making processes.

Research Interests

Information Exchange, Decision Support Systems, Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Business Analytics, Data Visualization

Research

My research develops frameworks for information exchange decision support, with particular emphasis on spatial decision-making and multi-stakeholder collaboration. I employ both quantitative and qualitative research methods to examine how information systems can support complex decision-making processes.

Current Projects

Information Exchange Decision Support (IEDS) Framework: Developing theoretical and empirical foundations for information exchange platforms that facilitate decision-making among multiple stakeholders using GIS technology.

Publishing Patterns Analysis: Empirical assessment of publishing trends in peer-reviewed journals across information systems and management disciplines.
