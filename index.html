About

Dr. Ali Bazarah is an Assistant Professor in the Computer and Management Information Systems department at Stonehill College. His research focuses on information systems, geospatial information exchange, decision support systems, and business analytics. He specializes in developing frameworks for information exchange among multiple stakeholders to support decision-making processes.

Dr. Bazarah has extensive experience in both academic research and practical applications of information systems, with particular expertise in GIS technology, data analytics, and system design. His work has been published in peer-reviewed journals and presented at major international conferences.
